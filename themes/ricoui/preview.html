Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Use #19191b as the default dark canvas background.
  • Set primary headings in 汇文明朝体 at 72px for the editorial hero feel.
  • Use #f7f5f3 for primary text to maintain high contrast.
  • Reserve #499ad5 for links, recommendation badges, and interactive accents.
  • Use #f7d252 only for primary CTAs and the Magic Click switch glow.
  • Apply 32px–36px pill radius for tags, filters, and CTA buttons.
  • Keep card images in a 2:3 ratio with 6px radius and a subtle 1px border.
Don't — Avoid
  • Don't use Huiwen Mincho for body text or UI labels — display-only.
  • Don't place saturated accent colors on large backgrounds.
  • Don't use sharp 0px radius on interactive components.
  • Don't let body text exceed a 480–640px measure.
  • Don't ignore the 8px base grid.
  • Don't render the 3D image trail on mobile.
  • Don't use pure white #fff as the default text color.
